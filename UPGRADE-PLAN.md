# Prism Global: Lagos property growth plan

The immediate goal is to turn property interest into a useful conversation: show a small, credible selection of Lagos properties, make the next step obvious, and give overseas buyers evidence they can review remotely. The recommendations below are proposed experiments, not promised traffic or sales results.

## Phase 1 — Publish a clear, trustworthy foundation

- Keep the homepage focused on available properties, remote buyer support, dated progress evidence and contact. Use one dominant enquiry action and a secondary property-browsing action.
- Give every genuine listing a permanent detail page with a descriptive title, location, asking price, availability and a property-specific WhatsApp enquiry. Maintain availability rather than leaving sold stock presented as available.
- Curate photographs in this order: exterior cover, living space, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, amenities, floor plan and access/context. Keep construction updates in chronological order with capture dates. Clearly distinguish actual photos, renderings and illustrations.
- Present the real company identity, accountable contacts and supported registration information. Explain document review status precisely; do not use an unsupported “verified property” badge.
- Check the deployed site's crawlability, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots rules and social previews. Submit the sitemap through an owned Search Console account, then inspect key URLs. Confirm that the actual hosting configuration serves property pages and the API correctly.

Google recommends discoverable HTML image elements, relevant surrounding text, descriptive alt text and filenames, responsive images and representative preview images. These steps improve the information available to search systems; indexing and ranking remain uncertain. [Google image SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images)

## Listing intake checklist

Before publishing each additional Lagos property, collect:

- Internal reference, seller/agent authority to market, property type and availability.
- Neighbourhood and accurate location context; decide the appropriate public address detail with the owner.
- Asking price in its actual currency, what is included, additional known fees, and any supported payment terms.
- Bedrooms, bathrooms, measured floor/plot area and units, parking, utilities and amenities; distinguish confirmed details from seller statements.
- Ready/off-plan/construction stage, source of the completion estimate and date last checked.
- Title/document description, documents received, review status, reviewer and review date where applicable. Keep sensitive originals out of public galleries.
- Exterior cover and ordered original photos, photo rights, captions, capture dates, floor plan and a walkthrough if available. Record whether each image is a photograph or rendering.
- Assigned contact, property-specific enquiry link and date to reconfirm availability and price.

The Lagos land portal offers property search and certified-copy services, while LASBCA provides a certificate verification page. Company registration, land title and building certification address different questions; one does not establish all the others. Link to relevant official services and support the buyer's independent professional review. [Lagos land portal](https://landonline.lagosstate.gov.ng/index.html), [LASBCA certificate verification](https://lasbca.lagosstate.gov.ng/lasbca-verify/)

## Phase 2 — Build confidence for buyers abroad

Publish a practical buying-from-abroad page explaining enquiry, video viewing, document review, agreed transaction steps and updates/handover. Describe only services actually provided. Offer a short property pack and optional call time/time zone instead of a long initial form.

Approved factual basis from the owner: trained vision models track progress and report when due. Suggested copy: **“Our trained vision models help us track construction progress and prepare reports when updates are due.”**

Before adding detailed reporting promises, confirm the cadence, delivery channel, projects covered, human review process and escalation owner. Prepare a real redacted sample report with source photo dates, observations, limitations, next expected update and a named contact. Do not invent continuous monitoring, model accuracy, structural certification or guaranteed completion predictions. A report schedule and authentic sample remain pending owner input.

A useful market example is The Diaspora Property Advisory Connect, which foregrounds documentation alongside property purchase and sales services. This is a presentation reference, not evidence that a particular layout improves conversion. [TDPAC](https://tdpac.co/)

## Phase 3 — Reach the right audience

Start a six-week content experiment:

- Publish two authentic property walkthroughs per month, each linking to its matching listing.
- Publish a dated construction update weekly when active work provides meaningful evidence; skip empty updates.
- Publish one practical Lagos neighbourhood or remote-buying guide based on firsthand knowledge and current inventory.
- Seek permission-based partnerships with Nigerian diaspora associations and relevant community publishers. Prepare the material before outreach; do not send unsolicited bulk messages.
- Keep an eligible Google Business Profile accurate, add current photographs and request authentic customer reviews. Google describes local ranking in terms of relevance, distance and prominence. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)
- Once enquiries are being handled reliably, test a small, explicitly approved campaign budget in diaspora locations supported by actual enquiry data. Send visitors to relevant property pages. Avoid premature assumptions about which countries will convert best.

Property portals illustrate familiar buyer expectations: visible prices, locality, property type, bedroom counts and accessible contact actions. Use those conventions without reproducing their content. [Nigeria Property Centre Lagos listings](https://nigeriapropertycentre.com/for-sale/lagos), [PropertyPro](https://propertypro.ng/)

## Measurement and review

Establish a baseline before launch and review weekly for six weeks. Record search impressions and clicks by page/query, listing views, WhatsApp clicks, submitted enquiries, qualified enquiries, booked viewings, time to first response and eventual sales. A WhatsApp click is contact intent, not proof of a received enquiry. Use campaign source tags and a simple enquiry record to connect traffic to conversations; keep personal details out of analytics events. Compare useful enquiries by source and property before expanding spend.

Owner inputs still needed: actual additional listings and media, supported business credentials, confirmed service commitments, a redacted model-assisted report, reporting cadence and an approved outreach budget.

## Deployment prerequisite

The current Vercel API allows unauthenticated saves, as identified during this project review. This requires separate authentication and authorization hardening before deployment. A visual upgrade does not resolve that issue. Protect write operations, verify server-side authorization and test rejected unauthenticated writes before making the upgraded site live.
