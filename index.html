A few things you may want to know.

Can you check a property you are not selling?

Yes. Share the property's location, seller details and available documents. We will confirm coverage, the proposed checks and fees before starting.

Can I arrange a viewing from abroad?

Ask our team about a live video inspection of your shortlisted property. Share your country and preferred call time so we can coordinate.

What do your vision models do?

Our trained vision models help track visible construction progress from site images and support scheduled reporting. The agreed service scope determines the images and updates you receive. Legal and structural checks require the relevant qualified professionals.

Should I still use my own lawyer and surveyor?

Yes. Retain independent legal and technical advisers. We help coordinate the on-ground work and evidence so you can make an informed decision.

Do you have properties in Lagos?

Contact us with your preferred Lagos area, property type and budget for current options. Published listings will appear in the property section as they become available.
