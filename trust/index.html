Verify the people, property and process before you commit

Prism Global expects serious buyers to ask hard questions. Use independent professionals, inspect the site and document every important commitment.

Direct answer

How should a diaspora buyer verify Prism Global and a property?

Do not rely on a website badge, WhatsApp message or verbal promise. Ask for current business and representative details, confirm the property and seller, inspect it live, and retain your own Nigerian lawyer and relevant surveyor or engineer.

Marketing copy is not proof of title, ownership, professional certification or guaranteed investment returns.

Verification checklist before payment

Identity and authority

Confirm who you are dealing with, who owns the property and who has authority to sell.

Title and survey

Give documents to your independent lawyer and surveyor for appropriate checks.

Physical inspection

Visit or request a live walkthrough showing the site, access and surroundings.

Written scope

Record fees, deliverables, dates, reporting, responsibilities and exit terms.

Milestone evidence

Tie building releases to dated evidence and review of agreed progress.

Payment trail

Use only the process confirmed in writing and retain every receipt.

Evidence you can request

Depending on the service, request seller-supplied property documents, current site media, live walkthroughs, a written scope, milestone reports, receipts and named project contacts.

Independent checks remain essential. Prism Global coordination does not replace legal advice, a survey, valuation or structural inspection.
