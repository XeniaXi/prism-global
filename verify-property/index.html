Before the first conversation

A property link or reference, location and a short description of your concern are enough to start. If you live abroad, include your country and a convenient time to speak.

Can I start while living outside Nigeria?

Yes. Send the property details by WhatsApp and discuss what can be coordinated remotely, with coverage and scope confirmed for your location.

What should I ask for before committing?

Ask for the available property documents, current site evidence, the seller's identity and authority, and a written scope. Your independent advisers can help determine the checks needed for your transaction.

Can progress images confirm construction quality?

Images can help show visible changes over time. Ask the relevant technical professional to assess workmanship, structural quality and any areas the images cannot establish.
